# AI-game_master
AI driven Interactive Story Agent

This game takes heavy inspiration from Dungeons & Dragons. It is a single player text-based rpg. The AI game master will autogenerate a scenario with a concrete overall objective. This objective is the win-condition. Once the scenario is generated, character creation will begin. Up to four characters can be created. A character's stats can be autogenerated based on their class, or the player can manually allocate the stats themself out of a pool of 20 points. Once all players are generated, the story can begin. The AI game master will prompt the next event and each character will make a choice on their turn. Each turn the player will have an ability roll, attack roll and damage roll. If a character is attacked, they can roll a saving throw to protect themselves. After every event, the game master advances the story until everyone dies or the win-condition is met and the players win.
